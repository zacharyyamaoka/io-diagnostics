Where there was no real signal:

  • A tool the host did not launch and that does not hold the lock gives no signal. A flash or tview started in a terminal shows only while the on-connect probe was blocked by it (the held cause); a tool that starts after the probe finished, on an adapter nobody is probing, is invisible until the host sees its holder. That signal exists only for plugins that declare a probe (moteus_servo does; a camera's held-by-realsense-viewer shows through the console cause only when RobotDx launched it).
  • The presence process's own blocked passes (BusLockHeld with holders, Connected only) are not used: they exist only while a page asks, so the dot would depend on a toggle. The probe's holders are the stable signal.
  • A row with no dot cannot pulse: a board that has not reported a check yet (presence null) has no dot to animate, so a run on it shows on its adapter and CAN bus only.
